# Customer Communication During Outages

## Channels

During a network outage the following channels are used, in order: the public service status page, SMS to affected customers, the customer support IVR message, and social media.

## Message content

Every customer message must state the affected area, the affected services, when the fault started, and when the next update will be published. Never promise a restoration time unless the incident commander has confirmed it.

## Business customers

Business customers with an SLA contract receive a direct call or email from their account team within 1 hour of a P1 or P2 incident being declared.

## Individual customer contacts

When a customer contacts support about a known incident, the agent links the customer to the existing incident instead of creating a duplicate, and informs the customer of the incident reference.
