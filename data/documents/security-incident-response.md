# Security Incident Response

A security incident is any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity or availability of systems or customer data.

1. Report the suspected incident to the Security Operations Centre (SOC) immediately.
2. Do not attempt to investigate compromised systems yourself.
3. The SOC classifies the incident. Incidents with customer impact are always Priority 1.
4. Personal data breaches are reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours and to the authority within 72 hours.

Credentials suspected to be leaked must be rotated immediately.
