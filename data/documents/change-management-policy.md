# Change Management Policy

All changes to production network elements require a change request.

- **Standard changes** are pre-approved, low-risk and repeatable, for example adding a customer VLAN.
- **Normal changes** require approval by the Change Advisory Board (CAB), which meets on Mondays and Thursdays.
- **Emergency changes** restore service during an incident and require duty manager approval; they are reviewed by the CAB afterwards.

Software upgrades on radio sites (gNodeB, eNodeB) are always normal changes and must include a tested rollback plan. A change freeze applies from 15 December to 6 January.
