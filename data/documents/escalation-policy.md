# Support Escalation Policy

## Support tiers

- **L1 Customer Support** handles first contact, identity verification, account questions and scripted troubleshooting.
- **L2 Technical Support** handles complex troubleshooting, provisioning errors and single-site network faults. L2 has two teams: L2 Mobile Core and L2 Fixed Access.
- **L3 Network Engineering** handles platform faults, vendor escalations and multi-site outages.

## When to escalate

L1 must escalate to L2 when a scripted troubleshooting runbook has been completed without resolution, when a provisioning system returns an error, or when more than three customers report the same symptom in the same postcode within one hour.

L2 must escalate to L3 when a fault affects more than one network site, when a vendor ticket is required, or when the incident is classified Priority 1.

## Time limits

- A P1 incident is escalated to L3 and the duty manager immediately.
- A P2 incident is escalated to L3 if it is not restored within 4 hours.
- A P3 incident is escalated to L3 if it is not restored within 2 business days.

## Business customers

Business customers with an SLA contract are always handled by the Business Service Desk, which can escalate directly to L3.
