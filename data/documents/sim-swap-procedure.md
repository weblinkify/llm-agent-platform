# SIM Swap Procedure

A SIM swap replaces a customer's physical SIM card or eSIM profile. SIM swaps are a common fraud vector, so identity verification is mandatory.

## Identity verification

Before any SIM swap the customer must pass strong identification: bank ID login, mobile certificate, or an in-store ID check. Knowledge-based questions alone are not sufficient.

## Free SIM upgrades

Customers on a legacy USIM card (USIM-LEGACY) who need 5G receive a free upgrade to a USIM-5G card. The new card is mailed within 2 business days, or the customer can switch to an eSIM immediately.

## Cooling period

After a SIM swap, outgoing international calls and premium SMS are blocked for 24 hours as a fraud control.
