# Service Credit Policy

Service credits compensate business customers when the contracted availability is not met.

- Availability below target by up to 0.5 percentage points: 10% of the monthly fee.
- Below target by 0.5 to 2 percentage points: 25% of the monthly fee.
- Below target by more than 2 percentage points: 50% of the monthly fee.

Credits must be claimed within 60 days of the end of the affected month. Consumer customers receive a pro-rata refund when a single outage lasts more than 24 hours.
