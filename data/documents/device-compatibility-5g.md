# 5G Device Compatibility List

Devices verified for 5G NR on bands n78 and n28:

- Apple iPhone 12 and newer (iPhone 12, 13, 14, 15)
- Samsung Galaxy S21 and newer
- Google Pixel 6 and newer
- OnePlus 9 and newer
- Nokia FastMile 5G Gateway (for 5G Home Internet)

Devices that do not support 5G: Apple iPhone 11 and older, Samsung Galaxy S10 4G and older, and all NB-IoT sensors.

If a device is not on the list, check the manufacturer's specification for band n78 support.
