# Major Outage Procedure

A major outage is any Priority 1 incident or any P2 incident expected to last more than 8 hours.

## First 15 minutes

1. The NOC engineer creates the incident and classifies it as P1.
2. The duty manager is paged through the on-call system.
3. A major incident bridge is opened and the incident commander role is assigned.
4. The customer communications team is notified to publish a service status message.

## Every 30 minutes

The incident commander publishes an internal update with impact, current hypothesis, actions taken and next update time. Customer-facing status pages are updated with the same cadence.

## Restoration

After service is restored, the incident moves to MONITORING for at least 2 hours before it can be closed as RESOLVED.

## After the outage

A postmortem must be completed within 5 business days for every P1 incident, using the Incident Postmortem Template. Service credits for business customers are calculated by the Billing team according to the Service Credit Policy.
