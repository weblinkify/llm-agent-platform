# IPTV Troubleshooting

1. Check for active incidents affecting TV-BASIC in the customer's city.
2. Confirm the set-top box is connected by cable, not Wi-Fi.
3. Restart the set-top box and check that the firmware is current.
4. If only some channels buffer, it is usually a multicast issue; escalate to L2 Fixed Access.

Catch-up recordings are available for 7 days.
