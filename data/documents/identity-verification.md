# Customer Identity Verification

Support agents must verify the caller's identity before disclosing or changing account information.

- **Basic verification** (customer number plus date of birth) allows the agent to discuss open incidents and general service status.
- **Strong verification** (bank ID or mobile certificate) is required for SIM swaps, number porting, changing the account owner and changing contact details.

Agents must never read out full payment card numbers or personal identity codes.
