# Business Customer SLA

Business customers with an SLA contract (for example Business SD-WAN, BIZ-SDWAN) receive enhanced targets.

| Service class | Availability | Restore time |
|---|---|---|
| Gold | 99.95% | 4 hours, 24/7 |
| Silver | 99.9% | 8 hours, 24/7 |
| Bronze | 99.5% | Next business day |

Business SD-WAN is delivered as Gold by default and includes an optional 5G backup link.

Availability is measured monthly per site. If availability falls below the target, the customer is entitled to service credits under the Service Credit Policy.
