# VoLTE and VoWiFi Troubleshooting

Voice over LTE (VoLTE) and Voice over Wi-Fi (VoWiFi) are included in all mobile plans.

1. Confirm that the device model supports VoLTE on our network.
2. Ask the customer to enable VoLTE and Wi-Fi calling in settings.
3. Reprovision the IMS profile from the provisioning portal.
4. If calls drop when moving from Wi-Fi to mobile, update the device software.

Emergency calls over VoWiFi send the customer's registered address, so the address must be up to date.
