# Account Suspension Policy

An account is suspended when an invoice is more than 30 days overdue after two payment reminders.

- A suspended account (status SUSPENDED) keeps emergency calling but loses outgoing calls and mobile data.
- No new services, plan upgrades or 5G activations can be made on a suspended account.
- The account is reactivated within 1 hour of the overdue amount being paid.

Support agents cannot lift a suspension; customers must contact Billing.
