# Fiber Installation SLA

- Apartment buildings already connected to fiber: installation within 5 business days of the order.
- Detached houses requiring new trenching: installation within 8 weeks, weather permitting.
- Business premises: installation date agreed in the order confirmation, normally within 20 business days.

If the installation date is missed because of the operator, the customer receives one month's subscription fee as a credit. Missed appointments by the customer can be rebooked once without charge.
