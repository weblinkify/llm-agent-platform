# Roaming Troubleshooting

## Customer has no data while abroad

1. Check that data roaming is enabled on the device.
2. Check that the destination country is in the EU/EEA zone or that a roaming pack is active.
3. Check the fair-use counter. Mobile 5G Unlimited includes 50 GB EU roaming data per month. The EU Roaming Pack (ROAM-EU) adds 30 GB.
4. Ask the customer to manually select a partner network.
5. If the problem persists, escalate to L2 Mobile Core with the visited network code (MCC-MNC).

## Bill shock protection

Outside the EU, data roaming is capped at 60 EUR per billing cycle unless the customer lifts the cap.
