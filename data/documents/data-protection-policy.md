# Customer Data Protection Policy

Customer personal data is processed according to the GDPR.

- Access to customer records is granted on a need-to-know basis through role-based access control.
- Personal data such as names, email addresses, phone numbers and personal identity codes must not be written to application logs.
- Personal data must not be sent to external AI services unless a data processing agreement is in place; masked data should be used where possible.
- Customer data is retained for 24 months after the end of the contract, unless law requires longer retention.
- Data subject access requests must be answered within 30 days.

Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours.
