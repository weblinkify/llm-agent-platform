# Billing Dispute Process

1. Record the disputed invoice number and amount.
2. Place the disputed amount on hold. The undisputed amount remains due.
3. Billing reviews the dispute within 14 days.
4. If the dispute is accepted, a credit note is issued on the next invoice.

Collections and account suspension are paused for the disputed amount while the dispute is open.
