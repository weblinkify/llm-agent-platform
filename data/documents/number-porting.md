# Number Porting

Incoming number porting requires strong identity verification and the customer's consent to terminate the old contract.

- Standard porting completes within 5 business days.
- Express porting completes on the next business day for an additional fee.

During porting the customer may experience up to 15 minutes without service. Outgoing porting requests cannot be blocked because of an outstanding balance.
