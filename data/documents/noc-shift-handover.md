# NOC Shift Handover

At every shift change the outgoing NOC engineer hands over:

- all ACTIVE and MONITORING incidents with their current owner,
- planned maintenance in the next 12 hours,
- open vendor tickets, and
- any customer escalations promised a callback.

Handover is recorded in the shift log. A P1 incident cannot be handed over without a verbal briefing to the incoming incident commander.
