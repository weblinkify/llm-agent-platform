# Incident Postmortem Template

Every P1 incident requires a blameless postmortem within 5 business days.

Sections:

1. Summary — what happened and the customer impact.
2. Timeline — detection, acknowledgement, escalation, mitigation and resolution times.
3. Root cause — the technical cause and contributing factors.
4. What went well and what went badly.
5. Action items — each with an owner and a due date.

Postmortems are shared with all NOC and engineering staff.
