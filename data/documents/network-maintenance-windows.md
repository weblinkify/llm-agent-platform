# Network Maintenance Windows

Planned maintenance that may affect customer service is performed between 00:00 and 05:00 local time, Tuesday to Thursday.

- Customers must be notified at least 7 days in advance of planned maintenance expected to cause more than 15 minutes of interruption.
- Business customers with an SLA contract must be notified at least 10 days in advance.
- Emergency maintenance may be performed outside the window with duty manager approval.

Planned maintenance does not count against incident SLAs if the customer was notified on time.
