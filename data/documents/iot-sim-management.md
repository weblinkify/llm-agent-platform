# IoT SIM Management

NB-IoT connectivity (IOT-NB) is billed per SIM.

- IoT SIMs can be suspended and reactivated in bulk through the IoT portal.
- NB-IoT does not provide 5G or voice. Devices that need high bandwidth must use a mobile plan.
- Coverage problems for NB-IoT sensors in basements are usually solved by enabling coverage enhancement mode.
